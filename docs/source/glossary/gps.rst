GPS
===

🤖 Satellite positioning,
which lets a station beacon its own position as a tracker.
A receiver reports as NMEA 0183 sentences over a serial port,
or through the gpsd daemon.

References
----------

* `NMEA 0183 <https://www8.garmin.com/support/pdf/NMEA_0183.pdf>`__ (Garmin summary)
* `gpsd <https://gpsd.gitlab.io/gpsd/>`__
* `NMEA 0183 on Wikipedia <https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/NMEA_0183>`__
