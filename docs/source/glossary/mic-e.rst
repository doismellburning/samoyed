Mic-E
=====

🤖 A compact :doc:`aprs` position format:
the latitude and a few flags ride in the :doc:`ax25` destination address
and the longitude, course and speed in a handful of bytes of the information field.

References
----------

* ``APRS101.pdf`` chapter 10 in `the APRS Documentation Project <https://github.com/wb2osz/aprsspec>`__
* `Mic-E types <http://www.aprs.org/aprs12/mic-e-types.txt>`__ and `examples <http://www.aprs.org/aprs12/mic-e-examples.txt>`__
